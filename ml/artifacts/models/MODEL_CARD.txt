THREATRAKSHAK TEMPORAL NEXT-STATE FORECASTER
============================================================

Purpose
-------
Forecast the next observed UNSW-NB15 attack-category state from a short ordered history of previous states.

Important terminology
---------------------
The nine UNSW-NB15 attack categories are used as network-state labels for this experiment.
They are NOT automatically treated as MITRE ATT&CK stages.

Model
-----
Embedding -> LSTM -> Linear classifier
Sequence length: 8
Best epoch: 30
Seed: 42

Data handling
-------------
Chronological train/validation/test progression artifacts were used.
No random shuffling was performed.
Original datasets were not modified.
Progression artifacts were not modified.

Validation results
------------------
Accuracy: 0.944062
Balanced Accuracy: 0.408969
Macro F1: 0.805864

Test results
------------
Accuracy: 0.936053
Balanced Accuracy: 0.336703
Macro F1: 0.751773
Weighted F1: 0.929389
Top-3 Accuracy: 0.985560

Interpretation warning
----------------------
These results measure next-state forecasting within the prepared UNSW-NB15 progression representation.
They should not be described as proof of real-world attack prediction without additional validation.
